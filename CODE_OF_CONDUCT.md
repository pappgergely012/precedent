# Code of Conduct

We want Precedent to be a welcoming project for everyone who contributes.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and kind.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Respect others' privacy: do not share personal information, private repository content or credentials.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block contributors who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (for example by messaging the repository owner from their profile); reports are handled with discretion.

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.
