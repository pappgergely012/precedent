# Project conventions

- **Language: always English** in this repository. All code, comments, documentation, commit messages, issues and PR descriptions must be written in English, regardless of the language used in the chat.
- This repo is public and open source: the rule-extraction engine, CLI and rule format. Never commit secrets, customer data or internal business information.
- Product name: **Precedent**. Related repos: `precedent-cloud` (private, SaaS layer), `precedent-internal` (private, business docs).
